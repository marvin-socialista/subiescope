**What's new**
- **OBD-II mode for newer cars (new).** SubieScope can now read cars the KKL cable can't, such as the 2015 and newer WRX and STI (VA), Levorg, XV / Crosstrek, Forester (SJ and newer), BRZ and GR86, and cars from other brands, with a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter such as the **Vgate iCar Pro BLE 4.0**. You get live gauges, logging to CSV, trouble codes with explanations (confirmed and pending), clearing codes, the VIN, and a boost gauge. Subaru-only values such as knock and IAM still need the cable and Subaru SSM mode. This mode has been tested with a simulated car and adapter: please tell me how it works on yours.
- **Setup wizard.** The first time you open SubieScope, a short wizard asks what car you have, recommends the right connection, walks you through connecting and testing it, and can start a demo car if you have no hardware yet. It is also in the Car menu.
- **Switch connection type in the sidebar.** The connection card now has an SSM cable / OBD-II switch and a device picker, plus a "Which one do I need?" guide with the supported cars for each. Each type remembers its own gauges. The demo car is no longer remembered between launches.
- **Better help when something goes wrong.** SubieScope keeps a log file, notices when it quit unexpectedly and starts without connecting automatically, and has Help > Send Diagnostic Report to bundle the log, your Mac model and any crash reports (personal details are left out).
- **More forgiving with quirky adapters and cars.** Values a car does not answer are skipped, adapters that can't read several values at once fall back to one at a time, and cars that won't list what they support are probed directly.
- **Universal app.** Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

**Install:** download `SubieScope.dmg` below, open it and drag SubieScope to Applications (replace the old version if you have one). You need macOS 14 or newer, and either a VAG KKL cable with an FTDI chip or a Bluetooth 4.0 (BLE) ELM327 adapter.
