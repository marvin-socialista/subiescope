**What's new**
- **Tactrix OpenPort 2.0 as the cable (experimental).** Have an OpenPort 2.0 from tuning your car? In Subaru SSM mode it can now take the place of the KKL cable: the Dashboard, the Logger, trouble codes, the troubleshooting tests and the dyno all work the same. It needs no driver. Take the microSD card out of the cable, plug it in and turn on **Settings > General > Tactrix OpenPort 2.0 cable**. **Car > Cable Setup** tests it step by step, and because the OpenPort measures the car's battery, it can tell a cable that is not in the car from an ignition that is off. This has not been tested with a real OpenPort yet, only with a simulated one, so it may not work on yours: reports are very welcome.
- **ROM editor (Advanced mode).** Open a ROM file (a copy of your ECU's tune), see its maps by name with their axes and real units, edit them, correct the Subaru checksums and save to a new file. It is hidden until you turn on **Settings > General > Advanced mode** and accept a warning, because a wrong change in a tune can damage an engine and is at your own risk. SubieScope never writes to the car: to put an edited ROM on the car you still need another tool.
- **Read the ROM from the car (Advanced mode, experimental).** The ROM editor can copy the ROM out of the engine ECU of the 2008 and later Subarus with a Denso SH7058 ECU, such as the 2008+ STI. It needs an OBDLink or other STN-based adapter in OBD-II mode, or a Tactrix OpenPort 2.0. A KKL cable or a plain ELM327 cannot do it. It only reads, and never erases or writes the ECU's program. It has not been tested on a real car yet, only against a simulated ECU, so it may not work on yours and you use it at your own risk.
- **Use your wideband gauge in the troubleshooting tests.** With an AEM gauge turned on, the tests that look at the mixture get a switch: "Read the mixture from the wideband gauge". The full-throttle pull, the knock check and the others then judge what your gauge measures instead of the car's own front A/F sensor, and the result says so. In the Front A/F sensor test the gauge is a second opinion, and the result tells you whether the two agree. "Analyze a Log…" follows the same switch.
- **Fixes.** Changing units no longer leaves the lowest and highest marks in the old units. A test that is running when the connection drops now ends with what it recorded. The connection panel tells you when the connection is lost, also with a log open. Replies from some OBD-II adapters were read wrongly and long replies could be cut in two. The search for extended values now tries every value. An empty boost cell no longer blanks a whole rpm step on the dyno, and log times read "0:03" instead of "0:003".
- **For developers:** `subiescope-cli rom` inspects a ROM file and corrects its checksums, and `subiescope-cli demo --openport` runs a simulated OpenPort.

**Install:** download `SubieScope.dmg` below, open it and drag SubieScope to Applications (replace the old version if you have one). You need macOS 14 or newer, and either a VAG KKL cable with an FTDI chip or a Bluetooth 4.0 (BLE) ELM327 adapter.
