**What's new**
- **Log an AEM wideband gauge (experimental).** Have an AEM wideband air/fuel gauge in the car? SubieScope can now read it at the same time as the car, so its reading is on the Dashboard, in the Logger and in the same rows of your log as rpm, boost and knock. It works with the SSM cable and with OBD-II. Turn it on in **Settings > Wideband** and pick the port: the gauge needs a USB to RS-232 serial adapter of its own, on its blue wire. For the X-Series (30-0300) and the UEGO gauges (30-4100, 30-4110). Tested with a simulated gauge only (the demo car has one, so you can try it without hardware): reports from real gauges are very welcome.
- **USB and Wi-Fi OBD-II adapters (experimental).** Besides Bluetooth 4.0, OBD-II mode can now use an ELM327 adapter on a USB cable or over Wi-Fi. Pick it in the adapter menu under "USB and Wi-Fi (experimental)". Tested with a simulated adapter only, so reports are welcome here too.
- **Extended values: SubieScope now asks the car what it has.** Some newer Subarus list the extended values they support. SubieScope reads that list, which makes the search quicker, and tells you when your car has values SubieScope has no name for yet. The ECU Info page also shows the car's ECU ID. Extended values might work on Subarus from about 2015, including cars with the FA20 or FA24 engine, but they are still untested on a real car: a report from **Help > Send Diagnostic Report…** helps a lot.
- **Update check.** Once a day when it opens, SubieScope looks for a newer version and shows what's new, with a button to download it. Nothing about you or your car is sent. You can turn it off in Settings, and **SubieScope > Check for Updates** looks whenever you want.
- **For developers:** the command line remote control can now also send the read-only Subaru SSM requests.

**Install:** download `SubieScope.dmg` below, open it and drag SubieScope to Applications (replace the old version if you have one). You need macOS 14 or newer, and either a VAG KKL cable with an FTDI chip or a Bluetooth 4.0 (BLE) ELM327 adapter.
