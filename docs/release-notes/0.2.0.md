**What's new**
- **Save and share your trouble codes.** A new Export menu on the Trouble Codes page copies every code, with or without its possible causes and fixes, or saves them as a PDF or text file to send to your mechanic or post on a forum. Right-click a single code to copy it, or use the Copy button in its explanation.
- **Knock check** (Troubleshooting, on the road): a short drive with a cruise, a few 4th gear roll-ons and an optional pull. You get a clear verdict on how much knock there is (None, Light, Moderate or Heavy), at which rpm and load it happens, what the ECU has learned, and whether hot intake air, a lean mixture or a hot engine played a part. Likely false knock is called out separately.
- **Clearer connection card**: a Connected badge, a Connect button when you are not connected, step-by-step instructions on how to connect, and cable detection that updates while you plug in. Connect to Car takes you straight out of log playback.

**Install:** download `SubieScope.dmg` below, open it and drag SubieScope to Applications (replace the old version if you have one). You need macOS 14 or newer and a VAG KKL cable with an FTDI chip.

Please report how it works on your car: https://github.com/marvin-socialista/subiescope/issues
